Straight answers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Old School Plumbing is licensed and insured. Our plumbers handle everything from routine maintenance to major repairs, installs and emergency plumbing.

Yes. Our team is on call as your 24/7 emergency plumber, because plumbing problems don't follow business hours.

What areas do you serve?

Kansas City, Missouri and the surrounding areas, including Grandview, Blue Springs, Lee's Summit, Overland Park, Leawood and Lenexa.

Yes. We handle commercial plumbing including restrooms, backflow, drains and water lines.
